# Taiwanese Mandarin beginner deck

The localized catalogue titles are **중국어(대만) · 초급**, **Taiwanese Mandarin · Beginner**, **台湾華語 · 初級**, and **臺灣華語・初級**. The deck uses Traditional Chinese and Taiwanese Standard Mandarin for greetings, transport, directions, meals and payment. Characters, phrases and examples describe 華語.

The 41 starter cards use selected everyday words and original example sentences. The range is practical rather than tied to a certification level. Forty headword pronunciations in Hanyu Pinyin and Zhuyin were matched to the Taiwan National Academy for Educational Research [April 2025 TBCL word list](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=41), offered under CC BY. The input sheet had SHA-256 `0cf6666bd1ed9af62b452bc5306bdc4d4520aead9c31b404e2065e8e075dfbad`. The pronunciation of 你好 uses the dictionary readings for [你](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=3026) and [好](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=80848). The cards show dictionary tones. Definitions, short examples, translations and related-situation links are authored for this deck.

[Editorial rows](taiwanese-mandarin-beginner.tsv) record the exact readings and source row IDs. From the repository root, regenerate the JSON with:

```sh
python3 tools/build-taiwanese-mandarin-beginner.py --published-at 2026-11-26T10:00:00+09:00
```

Use the application's catalogue and localization verifier for the canonical file. The HSK-based collections retain their own entries and source policies.

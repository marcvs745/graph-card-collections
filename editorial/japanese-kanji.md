# Japanese renewal deck kanji

Each deck adds one card for each distinct CJK ideograph in its Japanese vocabulary headwords. Example sentences, translated text and the repetition sign 々 are outside the extraction. A directed **Used in** link goes from the character to each matching vocabulary card in the same deck. Original vocabulary cards, IDs, order and edges are preserved. When a vocabulary card already has the same title, the character card adds a localized Kanji label to distinguish it.

Readings and historical forms follow the Japanese Agency for Cultural Affairs [Jōyō Kanji index](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/kanji/joyokanjisakuin/) where available. Readings outside the list and English glosses use [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/kanjidic/kanjidic_legacy.html), copyright the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used under the [EDRDG licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html) and distributed as adapted dictionary material under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The Korean, Japanese and Traditional Chinese glosses reuse the repository's reviewed Hanja or vocabulary clues where suitable, with Japanese-sense overrides for selected entries. Remaining adapted English glosses are labeled in the card text. Readings stay in kana, with pronunciation aids in approximate Hangul for Korean and rōmaji for English and Traditional Chinese. Japanese uses the kana directly.
